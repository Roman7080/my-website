Trail of Ten Falls

This beautiful hike features ten waterfalls along the trail in Silver Falls State Park in Oregon, with mossy rocks and lush forests creating a peaceful mountain atmosphere. This 8 mile loop takes you through beautiful forest and a variety of flora and fauna. The trail, as its name suggests, has ten unique waterfalls, each with its own beauty. The trail is moderately difficult, especially during rainy season when it gets pretty muddy. Dogs areallowed on the trail. Open year round, but it's recommended to go in the spring and summer. Autumn is also an underrated time to visit. Parking is good but the trail gets very popular at times, as this is one of the most well known hikes in all of Oregon. I would recommend anyone who is visiting to check it out.
