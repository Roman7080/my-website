About Us

I created this website to help individuals find which hikes are best for them. I've been a hiker for over 10 years. I've hiked all over the United States and in Canada. But I always wanted a site like this. When I couldn't find one, I decided to make one myself. It can be difficult to choose a hike that fits you, as there are so many different types of trails out there and different consitions that may not suit everyone. I know that there are many different types of hikers, and I wanted to create a website that would cater to all of them. Whether you're a beginner or an experienced hiker, we have something for you. Love winter hikes? We've got you covered with amazing alpine trails! More of a chill hiker? We have something for you too! I hope that our website will inspire you to get outside and explore the great outdoors!
